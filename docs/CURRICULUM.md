# SEBA AI Studio — Curriculum Mapping (LOMLOE)

This document describes how SEBA AI Studio aligns with Spain's LOMLOE curriculum framework (Ley Orgánica 3/2020, de 29 de diciembre, por la que se modifica la Ley Orgánica 2/2006, de 3 de mayo, de Educación) and how the 8 key competencies are integrated throughout the platform.

---

## LOMLOE Overview

The LOMLOE (Ley Orgánica de Modificación de la LOE) is Spain's current education law, enacted in December 2020. It establishes a competency-based curriculum framework that emphasizes what students can *do* with their knowledge, rather than what they merely *know*.

The framework defines **8 key competencies** (competencias clave) that all students must develop across their educational journey. SEBA AI Studio maps all educational content, practice questions, and AI-generated materials to these competencies.

## Competency Integration in SEBA

### Knowledge Bank

The LOMLOE knowledge bank contains curated questions organized by:
- **Competency** (1 of 8 key competencies)
- **Year Group** (Primària: 1r–6è, ESO: 1r–4t, Batxillerat: 1r–2n)
- **Difficulty Level** (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced)
- **Subject Area** (cross-referenced with specific subjects)

Each question includes:
- The question text (trilingual: CA/ES/EN)
- Four multiple-choice options
- The correct answer with explanation
- Competency tag and specific descriptor
- Bloom's taxonomy level

### AI-Generated Materials

When teachers generate materials (quizzes, crosswords, flashcards, etc.), the AI system:

1. **Receives competency context** — The system prompt includes the target competency's descriptors and expected learning outcomes
2. **Enforces alignment** — Generated content must demonstrate the competency, not just test factual recall
3. **Tags output** — Each generated item is tagged with the competency and year group
4. **Validates coverage** — The progress system tracks which competencies have been practiced

### Practice Sessions

Practice questions are served with competency awareness:
- Teachers can filter by specific competency or mix all 8
- The adaptive system tracks per-student competency mastery
- Weak competencies are prioritized in subsequent sessions
- Progress reports show competency-level breakdowns

### AI Chat (Aina)

The Aina teaching assistant is prompted with LOMLOE context:
- Responses reference specific competency descriptors when relevant
- Lesson planning suggestions map activities to competencies
- Assessment criteria align with competency evaluation rubrics
- The assistant can explain how any topic connects to the 8 competencies

## Competency Descriptors

### CCL — Linguistic Communication

The ability to communicate effectively in the language(s) of instruction and additional languages, using diverse modes and formats appropriate to context.

**In SEBA:**
- Reading comprehension questions
- Written expression activities
- Oral communication via voice features
- Multilingual content delivery (CA/ES/EN)

### CP — Plurilingual Competence

The ability to use different languages appropriately and effectively for communication, demonstrating intercultural awareness.

**In SEBA:**
- Trilingual interface (CA/ES/EN)
- Catalan dialect awareness (4 dialects)
- Cross-language vocabulary activities
- Cultural context in materials

### STEM — Mathematical, Scientific, and Technological Competence

The ability to use mathematical thinking, scientific methods, and technological tools to understand and transform the environment.

**In SEBA:**
- Mathematical reasoning questions
- Scientific method activities
- Data interpretation exercises
- Technology-integrated learning

### CD — Digital Competence

The safe, critical, and responsible use of digital technologies for learning, work, and social participation.

**In SEBA:**
- Digital literacy questions
- Online safety awareness
- Responsible AI use education
- Digital tool proficiency activities

### CPSAA — Personal, Social, and Learning to Learn

The ability to reflect on oneself, manage time and information effectively, collaborate with others, and maintain physical and emotional well-being.

**In SEBA:**
- Self-assessment activities
- Collaborative learning features
- Study skills and metacognition
- Emotional intelligence content

### CC — Civic Competence

The ability to participate in civic life as a responsible, critical, and committed citizen in a democratic society.

**In SEBA:**
- Democratic values activities
- Human rights awareness
- Environmental responsibility
- Community participation content

### CE — Entrepreneurial Competence

The ability to identify opportunities, develop ideas, and transform them into activities that generate value for oneself and others.

**In SEBA:**
- Creative problem-solving
- Project planning activities
- Financial literacy basics
- Innovation and initiative content

### CCEC — Cultural Awareness and Expression

The ability to understand and respect how ideas, emotions, and meanings are creatively expressed through various cultural forms.

**In SEBA:**
- Cultural heritage activities
- Artistic expression content
- Music and visual arts integration
- Catalan cultural identity materials

| Suficient | Developing | 50–64% |

Teachers can generate AI-powered reports that:
- Map student performance to competency descriptors

- Suggest targeted activities for improvement
- Align with official evaluation criteria

## Catalan Curriculum Specifics

SEBA AI Studio is specifically designed for the Catalan education system (Departament d'Educació, Generalitat de Catalunya), incorporating:

- **Catalan as vehicular language** — Primary instruction in Catalan with support for Spanish and English
- **Competències bàsiques** — Catalan-specific competency framework aligned with LOMLOE
- **Currículum d'Educació Primària** (Decret 175/2022)
- **Currículum d'ESO** (Decret 175/2022)
- **Àmbits** (knowledge areas) — Cross-curricular subject groupings used in Catalan schools
- **Situacions d'aprenentatge** — Contextualized learning situations as the primary pedagogical unit

## Data Sources

The LOMLOE knowledge bank is built from:

1. **Official BOE publications** — Royal Decrees defining competency descriptors
2. **Departament d'Educació** — Catalan curriculum decrees and guidelines
3. **XTEC resources** — Xarxa Telemàtica Educativa de Catalunya materials
4. **Teacher contributions** — Validated by educators and reviewed for accuracy
5. **AI-generated content** — Created by LLM, reviewed by bias scanner, approved by teachers

All content undergoes:
- Competency alignment verification
- Age-appropriateness review
- Linguistic accuracy check (CA/ES/EN)
- Bias scanning (weekly automated + manual review)
